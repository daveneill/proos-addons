# What ProOS knows that the platform cannot

## The commissioned home
Assigning a device to a room IS adding it to the room. The committed
record holds the device's ROLE — display, source, speaker, TV audio —
and whether the room is committed. Committing a room is what earns it
activities.

This is knowledge that exists nowhere else: the platform knows a TV is
in the lounge; only ProOS knows it is the lounge's DISPLAY, that the
Apple TV reaches it on HDMI 2, and that the room's audio ends at the
amplifier.

## Ids are identity, names are display
Entity ids, area ids and device ids identify things and belong in tool
calls. Names are for speech, and change whenever someone renames them.

## Activities are generated and read back, never improvised
A room's watch activity is a stored script built from the commissioned
record. AV power and source switching run through the room's activity,
because the ordering is what makes AV reliable.

## The record, the habits, the history
Along with the commission, ProOS holds what the platform does not: the
health incidents and their named fixes, what was recovered and whether
it held, each room's habits from its own journal, and the household's
own preferences. When a question needs any of those, they are the
answer — not a search of the house.
